## This website
I named my gamertag, ScaredRabbit(NL), after my rabbit Bikkel. He was a rabbit who was quite scared, although thats common for rabbits. I thought it was really resembling me as a person because I myself are often scared. Unfortunately, he died in august of 2025 having lived for over 6 years. I have decided to dedicate this website to him. R.I.P. Bikkel, May 2019 - August 2025.
